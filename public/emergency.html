Homeowner Emergency Protocol

What To Do Right Now

Take these 4 immediate precautions while our crew is in transit to your property.

01

Evacuate The Drop Zone

Stay clear of rooms directly beneath tree limbs. Structural weight can cause delayed collapses without warning.

02

Assume All Wires Live

If the fallen tree touched or pulled electrical lines, stay at least 35 feet away. Call Dominion Energy / NOVEC and inform our crew.

03

Take Safe Photos

Take wide-angle photos from a safe distance for insurance documentation. Do not climb on the roof or beneath the tree.

04

Call Dispatch Immediately

Call (703) 991-TREE so our nearest heavy crane truck is locked into your dispatch slot.
