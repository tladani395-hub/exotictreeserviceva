In most Virginia counties (such as Fairfax, Loudoun, and Henrico), residential tree removal on private property does not require a permit unless the tree is located within a designated Chesapeake Bay Preservation Area (RPA) or historic easement. Our ISA Certified Arborists will review your property plat and manage any required county filings.
